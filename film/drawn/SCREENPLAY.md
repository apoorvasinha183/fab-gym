# Sand to Chip: screenplay

*Draft v3 — 3 November 2026. Text-only rewrite for educational clarity, character performance and visual continuity. Supersedes v2 as a proposed creative draft; not yet synchronized to shots.json or voice.json.*

**Format and timing.** Thirteen sequences, 38 existing shot IDs. Existing v2 time ranges below are EDIT REFERENCES ONLY, not approved voice timings. Record a scratch read, allow reactions and process actions, then rebuild timing. Extend the ending rather than squeezing the recap and final reaction into its existing seven seconds. Target approximately 3 minutes 20 seconds to 3 minutes 40 seconds; confirm by performance, not word count alone.

**Audience and learning goal.** Curious beginners. Distinguish quartz, purified silicon, a single-crystal ingot, a blank wafer, patterned dies, and a packaged chip. This follows a simplified conventional silicon-chip route; there are other processes and packages.

**Characters.**
- GRAIN: curious, observant, occasionally dry; never babyish. Its recurring head tilt and eye construction carry identity through changes of material. Grain personifies the selected material; it is not one literal grain becoming an entire wafer.
- NARRATOR: an interested companion. Concrete verbs, clear explanations, room for silence. Never reads every visible label aloud merely to fill a pause.
- LOU: a meticulous, kindly robot guide. Small, confident gestures; checks the result before celebrating it.
- THE TWINS and CARBON: explanatory characters within the furnace/quartz views. Their departure represents chemical change, not moral wrongdoing.

**Visual direction.** Painted backgrounds with broad gouache-like shapes, warm paper colour and selective detail. Inked cels with subtly varied line weight, flat local colours, one designed shadow shape and restrained highlights. No glossy toy finish, uniform rim lights, gratuitous bloom or drifting camera used to substitute for action. Preserve deliberate metallic reflections where they explain a polished wafer or machine surface.

**Cohesion rules.**
1. Grain's eyes, brows, hands and characteristic head tilt follow one model sheet across forms. Face transfer is shown at the selected slice and selected die; do not suddenly multiply the protagonist.
2. Characters share the setting's light and contact shadows. Background contrast and detail soften behind faces and teaching actions. Do not hide missing integration beneath a film-grain filter.
3. Movement uses anticipation, a readable action, and settling. Hold drawings for thought; change them for action. The camera moves to reveal information.
4. Travel generally proceeds screen right. Vertical process movement is allowed; preserve its axis across cuts. Every shot has a handoff below.
5. Magnified scientific views retain a visible border and a small persistent “simplified view” marker. Inside them, material colours stay consistent. These diagrams are illustrative, not literal scale or atomically exact geometry.
6. Palette: sand and ochre → furnace amber/charcoal → purification lilac/white → crystal amber/indigo → wafer pearl/blue → fab blue-green → packaging cream/gold → beach warmth.
7. Introduce one technical term at a time. Keep subtitles separate from labels and clear of the teaching action. The end recap revisits known terms.
8. The headband belongs to Grain's coach avatar on a screen. Lou does not place fabric on a process wafer. Grain's physical material forms keep their clean silhouette.
9. Retain no unsourced purity percentage, universal process count or unexplained temperature. Any SMT2020 step count must be labelled as belonging to that simulated recipe. Put the simulator provenance in the end matter, outside the main explanation.

**Emotional progression.** Curiosity → separation → uncertainty → wonder at growth → hopeful misunderstanding (“Am I a chip?”) → learning the routine → apprehension at testing → relief → belonging.

## 0. EXT. BEACH - DAWN

**0-1** — v2 edit reference: 0.0 to 5.5 s

**NARRATOR**

A handful of sand. Hardly a treasure.

**GRAIN**

Depends what you find.

> **Continuity / handoff:** Sand form; alone on the fingertip. Hold the eye line toward the open sky; cut closer without changing sides.

**0-2** — v2 edit reference: 5.5 to 10.0 s

Grain peers over the fingertip, steadies itself, then looks out with growing interest. The title appears in the quiet sky. The hand stays still so the character supplies the movement.

**NARRATOR**

Come along. There’s something remarkable inside quartz.

*On screen:* FROM SAND TO CHIP

> **Continuity / handoff:** Sand form; alone. A pale quartz facet fills the picture. Match its shape to a quarry rock. This is a narrated change of setting, not the beach grain physically becoming a boulder.

## 2. INT. FURNACE HALL - FIRELIGHT

**NARRATOR**

> **Continuity / handoff:** Quartz form with Twins and Carbon in the explanatory view. Cut closer on Carbon reaching toward the oxygen figures.

**2-2** — v2 edit reference: 31.0 to 35.0 s

**GRAIN**

> **Continuity / handoff:** Grey silicon form; Twins and Carbon leave upward with the gas and do not return. Follow Grain’s downward glance.

**2-3** — v2 edit reference: 35.0 to 40.0 s

**NARRATOR**

> **Continuity / handoff:** Grey silicon; alone. Track its container right. Match the container’s outlet to the inlet of the chemical-processing sequence.

## 3. INT. PURIFICATION PLANT - PALE VIOLET

**NARRATOR**

> **Continuity / handoff:** Chemical-wisp form; alone. Follow the process line right into the distillation column; this is not elemental silicon simply evaporating.

**3-2** — v2 edit reference: 46.5 to 50.5 s

**NARRATOR**

Distillation separates it from unwanted substances.

*On screen:* DISTILLATION

> **Continuity / handoff:** Chemical-wisp form in the selected stream. Follow its pipe right to a distinct deposition chamber.

**3-3** — v2 edit reference: 50.5 to 56.0 s

**NARRATOR**

Another reaction deposits very pure silicon onto heated rods.

> **Continuity / handoff:** Solid purified silicon; alone. Follow one recognizable chunk right into a crucible. No icicles, frost, or freezing-from-gas transition.

## 4. INT. CRYSTAL-GROWING HALL - AMBER AND INDIGO

**4-1** — v2 edit reference: 56.0 to 60.0 s

**NARRATOR**

> **Continuity / handoff:** Silicon in the melt; alone. Follow its upward eye line to the incoming seed. Grain is not the seed.

**4-2** — v2 edit reference: 60.0 to 66.5 s

A separate, unpersonified seed crystal touches the melt. Show contact first, then slow withdrawal and rotation. Solid silicon grows at the boundary; Grain’s face reappears in this newly grown portion.

**NARRATOR**

A seed crystal touches the melt, then slowly rises and turns.

> **Continuity / handoff:** Growing-crystal form beneath the seed. Push into a magnifying window at the growth boundary; retain the same orientation.

**4-3** — v2 edit reference: 66.5 to 72.0 s

Within the window, incoming atoms join an ordered structure continuing from the seed. The attached crystal remains solid while liquid feeds the growing boundary. Use a clearly schematic lattice, not decorative circles presented as an exact atomic diagram.

**NARRATOR**

Silicon solidifies onto it, following the seed’s atomic pattern.

*On screen:* SIMPLIFIED VIEW

> **Continuity / handoff:** Growing crystal; alone. Pull back through the same window. Use a gentle time dissolve to make the long growth process explicit.

**4-4** — v2 edit reference: 72.0 to 77.0 s

**NARRATOR**

*On screen:* INGOT

> **Continuity / handoff:** Ingot form; worker distant. Show a supported lowering toward horizontal, then cut on that movement to the same orientation in the saw cradle. Do not jump directly from hanging vertical to unsupported horizontal.

## 7. INT. FAB TOOLS / EXPLANATORY VIEWS

**7-1** — v2 edit reference: 105.0 to 110.0 s

**NARRATOR**

*On screen:* DEPOSITION

> **Continuity / handoff:** Wafer with film; Lou outside tool. Keep the same cross-section orientation for the next instructional cut.

**7-2** — v2 edit reference: 110.0 to 114.5 s

**NARRATOR**

*On screen:* PHOTORESIST

**7-3** — v2 edit reference: 114.5 to 120.5 s

**NARRATOR**

*On screen:* LITHOGRAPHY

> **Continuity / handoff:** Patterned resist on the wafer. Match the revealed openings into the etching view; do not change their shape between shots.

**7-4** — v2 edit reference: 120.5 to 124.5 s

**NARRATOR**

*On screen:* ETCHING

**7-5** — v2 edit reference: 124.5 to 130.5 s

**NARRATOR**

*On screen:* DOPING

**7-6** — v2 edit reference: 130.5 to 134.5 s

**LOU**

Clean. Measure. Check.

> **Continuity / handoff:** Wafer; Lou outside. Lou’s final tap cues a time-compressed montage of further operations.

**7-7** — v2 edit reference: 134.5 to 140.5 s

**7-8** — v2 edit reference: 140.5 to 146.0 s

**NARRATOR**

> **Continuity / handoff:** Processed wafer in the physical scene; headband only on display avatar. Pull out from the schematic to the complete wafer. Keep that distinction through the rest of the film.

## Scientific basis and handoff notes

- Selected quartz, purified silicon, crystal ingots and wafer substrates: [Shin-Etsu](https://www.shinetsu.co.jp/en/products/semiconductor-silicon-business/silicon-wafers/).
- Chemical purification and deposition on heated rods in the Siemens process: [Wacker](https://reports.wacker.com/2022/annual-report/sustainable-solutions/purity-is-our-recipe-for-success.html). The wisp represents a silicon-containing chemical, not pure silicon gas.
- Seed pulling, ingot formation and wafer finishing: [SUMCO](https://www.sumcosi.com/english/products/process/).
- Deposition, resist, exposure, development, etching, implantation and inspection: [ASML overview](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/all-about-microchips/how-microchips-are-made) and [process explainer](https://www.asml.com/en/company/stories/2021/semiconductor-manufacturing-process-steps). The chosen examples do not prescribe one universal sequence.
- Dicing, die attachment, electrical connections, enclosure and final testing: [Semiconductor Industry Association](https://www.semiconductors.org/semiconductors-101/how-are-semiconductors-made/stage-4-back-end-manufacturing/).
