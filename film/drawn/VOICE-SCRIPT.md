# Sand to Chip: voice script (v2)

The narrator explains (warm, precise, quietly amused); Grain asks (curious, expressive); Lou keeps it precise (an affectionate, meticulous factory guide). Times are from a calm read at 2.5 words a second and settle with the read-through.

## Shot 0-1 (0.0 to 5.5 s)
*A painted beach at dawn; a fingertip lifts a little sand; Grain, one grain among them, peers up with its head tilted.*

- **Narrator** (0.5 s): Big things can begin very small.
- **Grain** (3.3 s): How small?

## Shot 0-2 (5.5 to 10.0 s)
*Grain's questioning look held against a quiet sky, then the title.*

- **Narrator** (6.0 s): Small enough to slip between your fingers.

## Shot 1-1 (10.0 to 17.5 s)
*From the beach to an ochre quarry with broad terraces; a selected quartz rock is lifted onto a conveyor, Grain riding on it.*

- **Narrator** (10.5 s): Many grains of sand contain quartz. Our factory journey starts with carefully selected quartz rock.

## Shot 1-2 (17.5 to 21.5 s)
*On the conveyor Grain peers at the rock; a magnifying circle opens over it.*

- **Grain** (18.0 s): What's inside it?

## Shot 1-3 (21.5 to 25.5 s)
*Inside the circle a simplified silicon-and-oxygen arrangement; the oxygen twins hold Grain's hands as the cartoon of the bond.*

- **Narrator** (22.0 s): Silicon and oxygen, chemically joined.

## Shot 2-1 (25.5 to 31.0 s)
*The furnace in charcoal, burnt orange and cream; the quartz goes in with carbon; Grain tumbles in with its twins and lands; the carbon characters wait.*

- **Narrator** (26.0 s): In a fiercely hot furnace, carbon helps remove the oxygen.

## Shot 2-2 (31.0 to 35.0 s)
*The carbon characters lead the oxygen twins away as departing wisps of gas; Grain, now grey silicon, calls after them.*

- **Grain** (31.5 s): There go my travelling companions!

## Shot 2-3 (35.0 to 40.0 s)
*Grain in its grey silicon form looks itself over and notices flecks of unwanted material on its body.*

- **Narrator** (35.5 s): What remains is silicon, but it still needs cleaning.

## Shot 3-1 (40.0 to 46.5 s)
*Pale violet, warm white and steel blue: Grain turns into its wisp form, the silicon-containing chemical, rising into a distillation column.*

- **Narrator** (40.5 s): First, silicon is converted into a chemical that can be purified by distillation.

## Shot 3-2 (46.5 to 50.5 s)
*The wisp rises through the column, the dark flecks left behind on the plates.*

- **Grain** (47.0 s): Leave the unwanted bits behind.

## Shot 3-3 (50.5 to 56.0 s)
*Glowing heated rods in a chamber visibly thicken as pure silicon deposits on them; Grain's face settles onto one rod.*

- **Narrator** (51.0 s): Then a reaction deposits very pure silicon onto heated rods.

## Shot 4-1 (56.0 to 60.0 s)
*Chunks of the rods melt in a glowing crucible.*

- **Narrator** (56.5 s): The purified silicon is melted.

## Shot 4-2 (60.0 to 66.5 s)
*A small seed on a rod touches the melt, then rises and turns slowly; the crystal starts to grow from the contact.*

- **Narrator** (60.5 s): A tiny seed crystal touches the surface, then slowly rises and turns.

## Shot 4-3 (66.5 to 72.0 s)
*A magnifying circle at the growth front: atoms joining in neat rows; then back to the chamber.*

- **Narrator** (67.0 s): Silicon solidifies onto it, following the seed's orderly atomic pattern.

## Shot 4-4 (72.0 to 77.0 s)
*The wide reveal: the tall ingot hangs from the puller in the hall, its size clear against the machine and a person; Grain's face is on the ingot itself.*

- **Grain** (72.5 s): I've grown.
- **Narrator** (74.1 s): One continuous crystal: an ingot.

## Shot 5-1 (77.0 to 81.5 s)
*Match cut from the hanging ingot to the ingot lying in the saw; a wire slices thin discs.*

- **Narrator** (77.5 s): The ingot is sliced into thin discs.

## Shot 5-2 (81.5 to 86.0 s)
*Follow one disc through a short montage: edge ground, polished, cleaned; one broad painted reflection; Grain looks into it, pleased.*

- **Narrator** (82.0 s): Their surfaces are carefully finished, polished, and cleaned.

## Shot 5-3 (86.0 to 93.0 s)
*The blank wafer enters a protective carrier with others.*

- **Grain** (86.5 s): Am I a chip now?
- **Narrator** (88.9 s): You're a wafer, the foundation for many chips.

## Shot 6-1 (93.0 to 97.5 s)
*Lou guides the closed carrier into a calm, orderly, blue-green clean room.*

- **Lou** (93.5 s): Welcome to the fab. Precious cargo coming through!

## Shot 6-2 (97.5 to 105.0 s)
*An inset enlarges a dust speck beside a delicate pattern; Lou gives the speck a stern look; the inset closes.*

- **Narrator** (98.0 s): Here, tiny patterns become electronic circuits. Even a speck of dust can spoil the work.

## Shot 7-1 (105.0 to 110.0 s)
*Grain in a deposition chamber; a magnified cross-section shows a new thin film forming across the wafer's surface.*

- **Narrator** (105.5 s): Thin films of material are added to the wafer.

## Shot 7-2 (110.0 to 114.5 s)
*The same cross-section: a distinct coating covers the film.*

- **Narrator** (110.5 s): A light-sensitive coating goes on top.

## Shot 7-3 (114.5 to 120.5 s)
*Light through a patterned mask changes parts of the coating; then the developer opens those areas, as two clear steps.*

- **Narrator** (115.0 s): Light transfers a pattern. Developing opens selected areas in the coating.

## Shot 7-4 (120.5 to 124.5 s)
*The same cross-section: material disappears only under the openings.*

- **Narrator** (121.0 s): Etching removes material through those openings.

## Shot 7-5 (124.5 to 130.5 s)
*A separate magnified view: a controlled, local addition of atoms; Grain watches with interest.*

- **Narrator** (125.0 s): Selected atoms can also be added to change silicon's electrical behaviour.

## Shot 7-6 (130.5 to 134.5 s)
*Lou does three short actions, one per word, inspects the result and gives a small nod.*

- **Lou** (131.0 s): Clean. Measure. Check.

## Shot 7-7 (134.5 to 140.5 s)
*The operations again with visible changes building up layer on layer.*

- **Grain** (135.0 s): And again?
- **Lou** (136.6 s): Many times. Each pattern has a job.

## Shot 7-8 (140.5 to 146.0 s)
*The montage resolves into a clear simplified circuit; Grain, satisfied, earns its red coach headband.*

- **Narrator** (141.0 s): Together, these steps form transistors and the connections between them.

## Shot 8-1 (146.0 to 150.5 s)
*A round wafer with a grid of dies; one die is highlighted; Grain points to it.*

- **Narrator** (146.5 s): One wafer holds many individual chips, called dies.

## Shot 8-2 (150.5 to 156.0 s)
*Into the highlighted die's magnified view; a simple demonstration of a switch turning a signal on and off.*

- **Narrator** (151.0 s): Inside them, transistors control electrical signals: tiny switches working together.

## Shot 9-1 (156.0 to 162.0 s)
*A hold before contact; fine probes meet the die's contact points, never its body.*

- **Narrator** (156.5 s): Electrical probes test the dies while they're still on the wafer.

## Shot 9-2 (162.0 to 166.5 s)
*A wafer map records results; Grain waits, gets its green mark, lets out its breath; failing dies stay on the wafer, marked.*

- **Grain** (162.5 s): Did I pass?
- **Lou** (164.1 s): You passed.

## Shot 10-1 (166.5 to 172.0 s)
*Overhead view following a cutting lane between the dies.*

- **Narrator** (167.0 s): The wafer is cut along the spaces between the dies.

## Shot 10-2 (172.0 to 176.0 s)
*Closer: one selected die, Grain's, is lifted out.*

- **Grain** (172.5 s): A little room of my own.

## Shot 11-1 (176.0 to 181.5 s)
*Warm cream and muted gold: the die is seated on its substrate, its connections made.*

- **Narrator** (176.5 s): A package protects the delicate die and provides electrical connections.

## Shot 11-2 (181.5 to 185.5 s)
*The protective housing closes; Grain's eyes keep their shape on the package.*

- **Grain** (182.0 s): So I can join in.

## Shot 12-1 (185.5 to 192.0 s)
*The packaged chip is installed on a circuit board.*

- **Narrator** (186.0 s): After final testing, the chip is ready to become part of a device.

## Shot 12-2 (192.0 to 199.0 s)
*Back to the beach: the phone wakes in someone's hand; the opening head tilt echoed by a tiny on-screen character; the sea and the last phrase breathe.*

- **Narrator** (192.5 s): From quartz, to crystal, to wafer, to chip.
- **Grain** (196.1 s): Small beginnings. Quite a journey.
