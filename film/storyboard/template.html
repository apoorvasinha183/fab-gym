From Sand to Chip

A short animated film in Three.js that follows one grain of sand all the way to a working chip. The middle of the story, inside the fab, is what Fab Gym simulates; the steps before and after it come from general knowledge and say so on screen.

The agreed direction

Chosen by the founder on 29 October.

Look: semi-real, with explainer animation

Physically based materials, reflections, soft shadows and depth of field, with Kurzgesagt-style explainers on top: labels with leader lines, a powers-of-ten scale bar, counters, molecule pop-outs and flow arrows. A mining scene opens the story.

Delivery: a video file

The scenes are rendered frame by frame in Three.js and encoded to one MP4.

Sound: voice and music

The narration voiced with Chatterbox on the Mac mini. Music: The Budding of Consciousness by Yoiyami under sand to wafers, crossfading into New Sunrise by nene as the box enters the fab and swelling at the finished wafer. Both CC0 from OpenGameArt; no attribution required, credited anyway.

Three.js

three@0.169.0 from npm (MIT), vendored with its licence: the module build and the post-processing, environment, geometry and utility add-ons.

Then, in order

  1. Storyboard stills. Done: each scene above shows a still rendered in Three.js, waiting for your sign-off.
  2. Scenes built one at a time, each playable on its own, starting with Layer by layer, the longest and hardest.
  3. A full cut with captions, rendered to MP4.
  4. Voice and music laid in and mixed to the picture.
The fab's part of the film runs on the SMT2020 model (Kopp, Hassoun, Kalir, Mönch 2020), a published research model of a wafer fab; its figures (583 steps, 105 tool groups, about 66 days for a regular lot) come from Fab Gym's own runs. The steps before and after the fab are general knowledge with typical figures. No real fab, company or product is shown. Music: The Budding of Consciousness (Yoiyami) and New Sunrise (nene), CC0 via OpenGameArt.
