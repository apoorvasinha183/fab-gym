# Art direction: Sand to Chip

The rules every picture of the drawn film follows, so that Grain and the rooms it stands in look drawn for the same
film, even in a paused frame. Chosen 3 Nov by quiz, after a second opinion found the first cut plastic: Grain glossy
and lit by nothing, laid over busy industrial rooms. The options compared are on a private page. The words Muse Image is given for each look are in
`film/drawn/looks.json`; this sheet is what they must produce, and what a contact sheet is checked against.

## The look: A, storybook painted

- **Backgrounds.**
  - Opaque gouache and watercolour with visible brush texture, broad simple shapes, soft edges and haze for depth.
  - The clearest detail and the strongest contrast go only where the eye should go, usually the middle.
  - Fewer pipes, panels and repeated parts than a real plant has.
- **Characters.**
  - Hand-inked, hand-painted cels: flat local colour, one soft shadow shape on the side away from the light, and at most one small highlight.
  - No gradients, gloss, rim light or vinyl sheen.
- **Light.** A character is lit by the room it stands in, using the colour script's light for that scene. The same words go into the background's prompt and the drawing's prompt.
- **Style pictures.** `film/drawn/looks/storybook-plate.jpg` is attached to every background request, so every room is painted alike. `storybook-cel.jpg` is attached when the model sheets are redrawn.

## The colour script: warm, cool, warm again

| Scenes | Time | Light |

| 8 reveal | 2:00 to 2:10 | dark with stained-glass beams, gold and violet |
| 9 test, 10 dicing, 11 packaging | 2:10 to 2:34 | soft test green; slate with warm sparks; board green and gold |

- **Colours carried through the whole film:** Grain's pale pink, the oxygen twins' red, and the coach's red headband.
- **Glow is saved for the big moments:** the melt, the crystal, the reveal and the phone waking.

## One model standard for every form of Grain

Every form keeps the same acting: sand, quartz, grey silicon, gas, crystal, wafer, coach, die and packaged chip. Only
its material and outline change.

| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| Eyes | Two tall ovals set close, a black pupil a third of the eye, one small catchlight; spirals only when dizzy |
| Mouth | Five shapes: closed smile, small "o", open shout, wobbly worry, wide grin |
| Hands and feet | Short rounded limbs in the body's own colour, mitten hands, round feet |
| Line | One ink colour (warm brown-black), thicker on the shadow side |
| Colour | Flat local colour, one shadow shape, at most one small highlight |
| Size | Each setting states Grain's real size and the camera's distance; a close-up is cut to, never scaled into |

## Acting

- **Key moments get full acting:** 6 to 12 drawings, with anticipation before the move, squash and stretch, travel on arcs, and follow-through (the eyes and the twins catch up a beat late).
- **Elsewhere:** strong held poses and small moves. No uniform bobbing and no sliding on one drawing.
- **The key moments:**
  - the tumble onto the belt (1-2);
  - the fall into the furnace and the landing (2-1);
  - the shout at the thieves (2-2);
  - melting into gas (3-1);
  - freezing into a rod (3-3);
  - the spin up out of the melt (4-2);
  - becoming the coach (7-5);
  - the reveal wave (8-1);
  - the "ouch" hop at the test (9-2);
  - the flight into the phone (12-2).

## The cohesion test

Before the other shots are repainted, three shots are made in the look and judged in paused frames:
- 2-1, the furnace: warm light, a fall and a landing;
- 4-4, the crystal: cool metal and a person for scale;
- 7-1, the wafer in the fab: clean-room blue.

The test passes when Grain and the background feel drawn for the same film and Grain is recognisable in all three
forms.

## Finishing

A grain filter or paint texture over the frame comes last, if at all, once the drawing, the light and the staging
agree.
