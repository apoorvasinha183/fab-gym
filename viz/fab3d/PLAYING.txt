FAB GYM 3D

Start with Take the guided tour, Walk and explore, or Fly over the fab.
Press Esc at any time for the pause menu, controls, comfort settings and places.
Go to place also gets you unstuck. Tour and exploration work without a helper.
DEMO means a recorded simulation from 06:00 to 06:30, repeated. A live connection
replaces it when the optional local helper is running. Model keys are optional.

Move: W A S D or arrow keys. Look: swipe with two fingers over the game view.
M (or the Trackpad look button) toggles one-finger trackpad / mouse look.
Press M again to release the pointer. Right-drag and I J K L also look.
In the browser, one-finger look keeps the cursor visible: move over the world
to turn, and press M again before using the map or other buttons.
Two-finger look follows your swipe. For a different system scroll preference,
choose Reverse two-finger swipe direction in the Esc menu.
Look sensitivity and vertical inversion are also in the Esc menu.
Shift moves faster.
F toggles flying; E / Q move up / down while flying.
The small map follows you: the blue arrow points where you face, gold marks Lou.
Campus / Nearby switches between the whole site and the local floor layout.
Choose a place below the map and press Go to travel to a safe arrival point.
The map stays north-up; its scale bar measures distance on the plan.
Click a tool to inspect it. Ask Lou opens a text box; Esc closes it.
Space finishes a line, then advances the tour.

Controller: left stick moves, right stick looks, X toggles flight.
Bumpers rise / descend, A interacts, Y advances a line, Start opens the menu.
Menus support Tab, arrow keys and Enter as well as mouse clicks.

Cinema: walk up to an empty seat and press E (controller A).
Enter (controller Y) plays the film. Space (Y) pauses / resumes it.
Esc (B / Start) returns to the hall. E (A) stands up.
1 / 2 (left / right bumper) choose a film while seated.
1 is Sand to Chip; 2 is Crime & Punishment. Captions appear automatically.
The cinema keeps the complete film frame when the window changes shape.

Desktop: unzip the download, then open the game. The macOS build is unsigned;
use the system's Open confirmation for this app. Linux: mark Fab-Gym-3D.x86_64
executable if your archive tool does not preserve permissions. Windows: run the exe.
No separate Godot installation is needed for the exported game.

Browser: publish all files in the web folder together on an HTTPS static host.
For local testing: python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory web
Then open http://localhost:8000. Opening index.html as a local file will not work.
A current browser with WebGL 2 and a keyboard/mouse or controller is required.
The single-threaded web build needs no cross-origin isolation headers.
The cinema films make the initial download substantial; allow it to finish.

For live simulation, run the repository's helper on port 8766. A hosted browser
build uses its own origin for live data; it remains in demo mode on a static host.
Speech and open-ended model answers need that live connection. The tour, tool
cards, local explanations and cinema are available offline.

This is the SMT2020 testbed in a layout of our own design, not a real fab.
