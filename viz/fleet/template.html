Rails and Vehicles

An hour of the fleet on today's US building, from the simulator's own trace (simulated day 2, seed 3000). The rails here are the ones the simulator moves vehicles on: the drawings' own rails: two one-way spine rails across the whole campus with crossovers, and in every bay a rail over each wall's load ports, joined at the far end. Every one of the 1,043 tools has its port under its wall's rail; stockers are served from the spine rail above them. Vehicles never pass one another, and one stopped to hoist holds up the vehicles behind it.

Held up means a vehicle is still on a stretch after it would have left at full speed: queued behind a hoist, a full stretch, or a merge.

Assumed

2.5 m/s everywhere, 3 m between vehicles, 60 s to load and 60 s to unload (cited from Yang 2023), nearest idle vehicle. The rails are the ones the plans, the BIM and the game draw, a design of our own; a test keeps the simulator's track equal to them.
