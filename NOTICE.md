# Notices and credits

## SMT2020

Fab Gym's world is the SMT2020 high-volume semiconductor manufacturing testbed:

D. Kopp, M. Hassoun, A. Kalir and L. Mönch, "SMT2020: A Semiconductor Manufacturing Testbed", IEEE Transactions on Semiconductor Manufacturing, 2020.

The revision used is the one published in the GitHub repository `prosysscience/PySCFabSim-release`, under that repository's MIT licence (copyright: Research Group Production Systems). Any copy of its files in this repository keeps that notice. That revision has 105 tool groups, 1,043 tools and routes of 583 and 343 steps; the paper reports 1,054 tools and routes of 632 and 374 steps for its own revision.

## Meta models

Fab Gym's agent runs on Muse Spark Open (open weights) served through Baseten, and uses Muse Voice Transcribe through the Meta Model API.
